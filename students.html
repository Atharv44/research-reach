Find me a lab

Only our team sees your profile. A lab sees it only with your approval

What you submit

A single form, about 5 minutes. It asks for

  • Name and IU emailWe use your IU email to reach you when there is a match
  • Class standing and graduation termMany labs recruit early, so freshmen and sophomores are welcome
  • Majors, minors and GPAGPA is optional, but it gives us more to match on
  • When you are freeHours a week, the days and terms that work and anything that limits your time
  • Paid, credit or bothAnd whether you can hold a paid campus job, so we never match you to one you cannot take
  • Courses you have takenBiology, Chemistry, Psychology and Brain Sciences and Physics, including this term
  • Interests and experienceThe areas you want to work in and any lab work so far. Experience is not required

Have these ready

  1. Your resume as a PDF
  2. Your class schedule for this term
  3. An unofficial transcript as a PDF
  4. The courses you have taken

What happens next

  1. 1

    We screen for fit

    We check your profile against what each lab asked for

  2. 2

    We email you a match

    When a lab fits, the introduction comes to your IU email. Nothing to do in between

Questions students ask

Do I need research experience to apply?

No. If you have some, that is a plus. We also look at your interests, your schedule and the courses you have taken

Does applying get me a spot in a lab?

We introduce you to a lab that fits what you asked for. The lab makes the final call, and we will be rooting for you

I’m a freshman. Is it too early to apply?

Not at all. Faculty told us they love training students early, so your first year is a great time to start
