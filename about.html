We make the match

Research Reach matches undergraduates with research labs that need them, starting at Indiana University Bloomington. Students complete one five-minute profile instead of sending cold emails. Faculty tell us once what they look for, and lab details stay private. We screen every student for fit, then make the introduction.

Why we started

Most students who want research hear about it late, and the only way in is a cold email to a professor they have never met. Most of those emails go unanswered.

Labs want students early, because training a new student takes up to two months. Faculty lose hours sorting inboxes full of students who don’t fit what they need.

Getting into a lab should come down to fit. Knowing who to email shouldn’t matter.

What we hold to

Fit over volume
We introduce a student only when they meet what the lab told us it looks for. We never forward a stack of applicants.
Same door for everyone
Every student fills out the same form. We never ask about race, ethnicity, first-generation status or income on the matching form.
Faculty time is precious
Joining takes one short email. Lab details stay private, and every email we send a professor has one ask.
