Website terms

01Who we are

Research Reach LLC ("Research Reach", "we", "us") is an Indiana limited liability company that matches undergraduate students with faculty research positions. These terms cover your use of our website, our student intake form, our faculty intake and the introductions we make, together called the service.

Research Reach LLC is an independent company. It is not part of Indiana University and does not act on its behalf.

02Agreeing to these terms

By using the service you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the service.

Students who submit the intake form also accept the Student Consent and Release Agreement below. Faculty who submit the faculty intake also accept the Mutual Data Sharing and Confidentiality Agreement below. Where one of those agreements and these terms differ, the agreement controls for that topic.

03Who can use the service

Students must be at least 18 years old and currently enrolled as an undergraduate student. Faculty take part as individuals and not on behalf of Indiana University.

04What the service does

Faculty tell us what their positions need. Students submit a profile. We compare the two and share the profiles of students who appear to fit with participating faculty, under the agreements below.

We do not guarantee that any student will be matched, interviewed or offered a position, or that any faculty member will receive a student. All interview, selection and hiring decisions belong to the faculty member. Research Reach is not the employer of any student, and the lab sets the terms of any position, including pay or course credit.

05What we ask of you

Give us accurate information and share only information that is yours to share. Do not submit Social Security numbers, IU University ID numbers, financial or bank information, health information or other sensitive personal information.

Do not use the service to mislead anyone, to harass anyone, to collect information about other people or to interfere with how the service runs. We may decline or stop providing the service to anyone who breaks these terms.

06Your information

We share student information only with the student's consent, and only with participating faculty and the lab personnel directly involved in selecting students. Every faculty member must accept the Mutual Data Sharing and Confidentiality Agreement before receiving any student information.

We do not sell student or faculty information or use it for advertising. We do not show faculty information to students or publish it.

Students may withdraw consent at any time by writing to our team. We then stop sharing that student's information and delete it within 14 days. Faculty may ask us to delete their information, and we delete it within 14 days of the request.

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use this site. Clarity records anonymized page interactions and masks what people type. We do not sell or share this data.

07Other services we rely on

Parts of the service run on tools from other companies, such as online forms and email. Your use of those tools is also subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

08Our content

The Research Reach name, logo, characters, text and design belong to Research Reach LLC. You may not copy or reuse them without our written permission. The information you submit stays yours.

09Cost

The service is free for students and free for faculty. If that changes we will say so before any charge applies, and nothing you have already done will be billed.

10No warranties

The service is provided as is and as available. To the fullest extent the law allows, we make no warranties of any kind, whether express or implied, including that the service will be uninterrupted, error free or suited to a particular purpose. We do not verify the accuracy of information students provide.

To the fullest extent the law allows, Research Reach LLC and its members, managers and agents are not liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for any loss arising from an introduction, a selection or hiring decision, or the conduct of any student, faculty member or lab.

Student Consent and Release Agreement

Research Reach Student Matching Program

This Student Consent and Release Agreement (the "Agreement") is between Research Reach LLC, an Indiana limited liability company ("Research Reach," "we," or "us"), and the student who accepts it ("you"). Please read it in full before submitting the Research Reach intake form.

01Purpose and Independence

Research Reach matches IU undergraduate students with faculty research positions. This Agreement explains what information you share with us, who we share it with, how we protect it, and what you agree to by taking part.

Research Reach is an independent company. It is not part of Indiana University ("IU") and does not act on IU's behalf. Taking part is voluntary. Choosing not to take part, or withdrawing later, has no effect on your enrollment, grades, or standing at IU.

02Eligibility

To take part, you must be at least eighteen (18) years old and currently enrolled as an undergraduate student.

03Information You Share

You may share your name, IU email, major, class year, research interests, skills, availability, resume, and, if you choose, your GPA or a transcript. Transcript upload is optional.

Do not submit your Social Security number, IU University ID number, financial or bank information, health information, or any other sensitive personal information. Research Reach will delete any such information it receives.

You are responsible for making sure the information you submit is accurate and is yours to share.

04Your Consent to Share Your Information

By accepting this Agreement, you give Research Reach your signed and dated consent to disclose the information you submit, as follows:

  • (a) What is shared: the information you submit through the Research Reach intake form, including any transcript you choose to upload.
  • (b) Why it is shared: so participating faculty can consider you for research positions in their labs.
  • (c) Who receives it: participating IU faculty members and the lab personnel directly involved in selecting students.

You understand your rights under the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), and you give this consent consistent with 34 C.F.R. § 99.30. To the extent FERPA applies to your information, you waive your right to prevent the specific disclosure described in this Section, and no other.

Research Reach will not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone outside the scope of this consent.

05How Research Reach Protects Your Information

Research Reach stores your information only in IU managed storage approved by University Information Technology Services ("UITS"), limits access to team members who need it for matching, and holds it to the standard FERPA and IU policy set for education records. Research Reach follows its Data Governance Charter, available on request.

Every faculty member who receives your information must first accept the Research Reach Mutual Data Sharing and Confidentiality Agreement. That agreement requires them to keep your information confidential, use it only for selection, never forward it, and delete it after the matching period unless they select you.

06Retention and Withdrawal

Research Reach keeps your information through the end of the academic year in which you submit it, then deletes it unless you ask to stay in the program.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by emailing Research Reach. Research Reach will stop sharing your information, delete it within fourteen (14) days. Withdrawal does not undo a disclosure made before your request.

07Responsibility After Your Information Is Shared

Once a faculty member receives your information, that faculty member is responsible for how it is handled, under their agreement with Research Reach, IU policy, and applicable law. Research Reach is not responsible for how a faculty member or lab uses, stores, or discloses your information after receiving it.

08No Guarantees

Research Reach does not guarantee that you will be matched, interviewed, or offered a position. All interview, selection, and hiring decisions belong solely to the faculty member. Research Reach is not your employer, and the terms of any position, including pay or course credit, are set by the lab.

09Nondiscrimination

Research Reach operates in accordance with IU's Non-Discrimination Policy (UA-01) and applicable federal and state civil rights law. Research Reach matches students solely on faculty requirements and the information you submit, and does not consider any characteristic protected under UA-01 or applicable law.

Concerns about discrimination may be reported to IU's Office of Civil Rights Compliance at ocrc@iu.edu.

10Incidents

If Research Reach learns that your information was accessed or disclosed without authorization while in its possession, it will report the incident to it-incident@iu.edu and notify you by email without unreasonable delay.

11Release and Limitation of Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, you release Research Reach LLC and its members, managers, and agents from any claim arising out of (a) any faculty member's or lab's handling of your information after receiving it, (b) any interview, selection, or hiring decision, or (c) your experience in any lab or position.

Research Reach provides this service free of charge and "as is." To the fullest extent permitted by law, Research Reach will not be liable for any indirect, incidental, or consequential damages, and its total liability to you will not exceed one hundred dollars ($100). Nothing in this Agreement limits liability that cannot be limited under applicable law.

12Changes to This Agreement

Research Reach may update this Agreement and will notify you by email. Any change to what is shared, why, or with whom requires your new consent before it applies to you.

13Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Indiana, without regard to its conflict of laws rules. Any dispute will be resolved exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Monroe County, Indiana.

14Contact

Questions and withdrawal requests go to our team.

Mutual Data Sharing and Confidentiality Agreement

Research Reach Faculty Partner Program

This Mutual Data Sharing and Confidentiality Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date defined below between Research Reach LLC, an Indiana limited liability company ("Research Reach," "we," or "us"), and the faculty member who accepts it ("Faculty Member" or "you"). Research Reach and the Faculty Member are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

01Purpose and Independence

Research Reach matches undergraduate students with faculty research positions. The Parties share information under this Agreement solely to identify and evaluate students for the Faculty Member's research positions.

Research Reach is an independent company. It is not a part, unit, agent, or contractor of Indiana University ("IU"), and it does not act on IU's behalf. Research Reach is not an IU school official and has no access to IU education records or IU student information systems.

02Compliance with FERPA, IU Policy, and Law

Each Party will comply with all applicable federal, state, and local law and all applicable IU policies in performing this Agreement, including:

  • (a) the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), 20 U.S.C. § 1232g, and its regulations at 34 C.F.R. Part 99;
  • (b) IU Policy DM-01, Management of Institutional Data, and its standards;
  • (c) IU Policy DM-02, Disclosing Institutional Information to Third Parties; and
  • (d) IU Policy UA-01, Non-Discrimination.

Although Research Reach is not part of IU, it holds all Student Data to the standard FERPA and IU policy set for education records. Research Reach will follow written guidance from IU's Student Data Steward, University Information Policy Office, and UITS on how Student Data is collected, stored, and shared.

Where this Agreement differs from FERPA, IU policy, or applicable law, the stricter requirement controls. Research Reach will update this Agreement as needed to stay consistent with them.

03Definitions

"Faculty Data" means information about the Faculty Member's lab, research, and open positions that the Faculty Member provides to Research Reach.

"Student Data" means any information about a student that Research Reach provides to the Faculty Member, including a student's profile, resume, and any transcript the student chooses to share.

"Transfer Point" means the moment the Faculty Member receives any Student Data from Research Reach. From the Transfer Point onward, the Faculty Member is solely responsible for the Student Data received.

"Matching Period" begins on the Effective Date and ends on the earliest of (a) the date all of the Faculty Member's listed positions are filled or withdrawn, (b) ninety (90) days after Research Reach first sends Student Data to the Faculty Member, or (c) the date either Party terminates this Agreement.

"Data Governance Charter" means Research Reach's written policy on data access, storage, retention, and incident response, as updated from time to time and available on request.

04Research Reach's Handling of Faculty Data

Research Reach will use Faculty Data only to match students with the Faculty Member's positions. Research Reach will not sell, publish, or disclose Faculty Data outside its team, show it to students, or use the Faculty Member's name in outreach without the Faculty Member's prior written consent.

Research Reach will store Faculty Data only in IU managed storage approved by University Information Technology Services ("UITS"), limit access to Research Reach team members who need it for matching, and delete Faculty Data within fourteen (14) days of the Faculty Member's written request or the end of the Matching Period, whichever comes first.

05Faculty Member's Handling of Student Data

The Faculty Member must keep all Student Data confidential and use it only to evaluate candidates for the Faculty Member's own research positions. The Faculty Member may share Student Data only with lab personnel directly involved in selecting students, and must ensure those personnel follow this Section.

The Faculty Member must not forward, disclose, or publish Student Data to any other faculty member, lab, department, or outside party, and must not use it for any other purpose.

From the Transfer Point onward, the Faculty Member is responsible for handling Student Data in compliance with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), IU policy including DM-01, Management of Institutional Data, Section 2 of this Agreement, and all other applicable law.

The Faculty Member must delete all Student Data within fourteen (14) days after the Matching Period ends. This deletion requirement does not apply to a student the Faculty Member selects, whose information then becomes part of the lab's ordinary records under IU policy.

06How the Process Works

The Faculty Member provides position requirements through the Research Reach lab template or a call. Research Reach sends qualified candidates by the date stated in the position listing; late or revised requirements may move that date.

Research Reach checks in when a match is made and again approximately one month after the selected student begins working in the lab. The Faculty Member agrees to respond to candidates within a reasonable time and to notify Research Reach when a position is filled or withdrawn.

07No Guarantees

Research Reach makes a good faith effort to identify students who meet the Faculty Member's requirements. Research Reach does not guarantee the number, availability, qualifications, conduct, or performance of any student, or the accuracy of any information a student provides.

All interview, selection, and hiring decisions belong solely to the Faculty Member, who is never obligated to accept any candidate.

08Student Consent and Data Minimization

Research Reach shares a student's information only after that student gives signed and dated electronic consent through the Research Reach intake form, consistent with 34 C.F.R. § 99.30. The consent identifies the information to be shared, the purpose of sharing it, which is consideration for faculty research positions, and the participating faculty who may receive it. The form links to FERPA and sends the student a copy of the submission.

Research Reach uses each student's information only within the scope of that consent. A student may withdraw consent at any time by written notice to Research Reach, and Research Reach will then stop sharing that student's information and delete it within fourteen (14) days.

Research Reach collects only the information needed to make a match. The intake form instructs students not to submit Social Security numbers, IU student ID numbers, financial information, health information, or other sensitive personal information. Transcript upload is optional.

09Data Security and Storage

Research Reach stores Student Data and Faculty Data only in IU managed storage approved by UITS, protected by access restrictions and two-step verification, and transmits it only through IU provided email or storage. Access is limited to Research Reach team members who need it for matching.

Research Reach retains, deletes, and protects data as set out in its Data Governance Charter.

10Incident Reporting

If either Party learns of any actual or suspected unauthorized access to, disclosure of, or misuse of Student Data or Faculty Data (an "Incident"), that Party must first report it to IU's IT incident team at it-incident@iu.edu, then notify the other Party in writing within forty-eight (48) hours of discovery.

The Party responsible for the data at the time of the Incident must take prompt steps to contain it and must cooperate with the other Party and IU. Suspected FERPA violations involving Student Data held by the Faculty Member must also be reported through the Faculty Member's ordinary IU reporting channels.

Notices to Research Reach go to our team.

11Disclaimer of Warranties

Research Reach provides its matching service "as is" and "as available." To the fullest extent permitted by law, Research Reach disclaims all warranties, express or implied, including warranties of fitness for a particular purpose, accuracy, and non-infringement. Research Reach does not verify the accuracy or completeness of information students provide.

12Limitation of Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, Research Reach, its members, managers, and agents will not be liable for (a) any act or omission of the Faculty Member, lab personnel, or any student; (b) any use, disclosure, loss, or misuse of Student Data after the Transfer Point; (c) any interview, selection, hiring, supervision, or employment decision; or (d) any indirect, incidental, consequential, special, or punitive damages.

Because Research Reach provides its service free of charge, its total liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement will not exceed one hundred dollars ($100). These limits do not apply to liability that cannot be limited under applicable law.

13Responsibility and Indemnification

Each Party is responsible for its own acts and omissions and for the data in its possession. The Faculty Member agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Research Reach, its members, managers, and agents from claims, losses, and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, to the extent they arise from (a) the Faculty Member's or lab personnel's breach of Section 2 or Section 5, (b) any use or disclosure of Student Data after the Transfer Point, or (c) any selection or employment decision the Faculty Member makes.

Research Reach agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the Faculty Member from claims, losses, and expenses to the extent they arise from Research Reach's breach of Sections 4, 8, or 9 before the Transfer Point.

The Parties are independent of one another. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, agency, partnership, or joint venture relationship. Neither Party may bind the other or act on the other's behalf. The Faculty Member accepts this Agreement as an individual and not on behalf of IU, and this Agreement creates no obligation for IU.

15Nondiscrimination

Each Party will comply with IU's Non-Discrimination Policy (UA-01) and all applicable federal and state civil rights law. Research Reach matches students solely on the requirements the Faculty Member provides and the information students submit, and does not consider any characteristic protected under UA-01 or applicable law.

Concerns about discrimination may be reported to IU's Office of Civil Rights Compliance at ocrc@iu.edu.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Indiana, without regard to its conflict of laws rules. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement will be resolved exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Monroe County, Indiana.
