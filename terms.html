04What the service does

Labs tell us what they are looking for. Students fill out a profile. We compare the two and introduce a lab to the students who appear to fit.

An introduction is not an offer. We do not guarantee that any student will receive a research position or that any lab will receive a student. Each lab decides who joins it, and each student decides whether to accept.

We are not a party to any arrangement between a student and a lab, including pay, course credit, hours, supervision or the work itself.
